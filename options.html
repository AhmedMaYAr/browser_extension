PubMed Article Explorer — Settings

NCBI E-utilities (optional, but recommended)

An NCBI API key raises your rate limit from 3 to 10 requests/second, so searches and related-article lookups run faster. Get a free key from your NCBI account settings.

AI-enhanced reports (optional)

Without a key, each article gets a quick summary extracted directly from its abstract — no external calls. Add your own Anthropic API key to also get a one-click AI-written report (background / key finding / relevance). The key is stored only in this browser and is sent solely to api.anthropic.com when you click "AI report". Get a key from the Anthropic Console.
